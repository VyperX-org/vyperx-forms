Role Overview - Business Development Associate (BDA) – VyperX

  • Generate leads through online (Instagram, LinkedIn) and offline channels
  • Pitch VyperX services: Social Media Management, Content Shoots, and Branding
  • Convert leads into paying clients and maintain client relationships
  • Conduct client meetings by calls and face-to-face interactions
  • Coordinate with content, editing, and creative teams to deliver client work
  • Drive repeat business through follow-up and strong account ownership

Eligibility Criteria

  • Commerce / BBA background preferred, but not mandatory
  • Prior experience in sales, business development, or team handling is a plus
  • Freshers with strong sales skills can apply

Required Skills

  • Strong communication skills in English and Hindi; Bengali is a plus
  • Confident in client handling for calls, meetings, and video pitches
  • Basic understanding of digital marketing, reels, and branding
  • Ability to generate leads and build a sales pipeline
  • Negotiation and closing skills

Additional Advantage

  • Camera-friendly personality and comfort in front of the camera
  • Ability to represent the brand in videos and content

Key Traits

  • Target-driven and result-oriented
  • Strong follow-up and persuasion skills
  • Street-smart and proactive mindset
  • High ownership and accountability
  • Comfortable with rejection and pressure

Performance Expectations (KPI)

  • Daily: 30–50 outreach activities through calls and DMs
  • Weekly: 5–10 client meetings
  • Monthly revenue target: ₹1L – ₹1.5L

Compensation Structure

  • Fixed salary: ₹8,000 – ₹15,000
  • Incentives: 10% – 20% per deal
  • Performance-based bonuses on target achievement

Location

  • Rajarhat, Kolkata (On-site)

Sales Intern - Lead Generation & On-Call

  • Support outreach, follow-ups, and client communication on calls
  • Assist the BDA team with lead qualification and meeting coordination

Final Note

  • We are looking for someone who can bring real business, not just attend meetings
  • If you are confident in closing deals and growing with a fast-paced startup, this role is for you
